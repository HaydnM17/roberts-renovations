01What we do

Renovations and repairs, inside and out

We take on full bathroom and kitchen renovations, exterior work like decks and siding, and the fixture, fitting and repair jobs in between.

  • Bathrooms
  • Kitchens
  • Tile
  • Flooring
  • Drywall
  • Exterior

  • Bathrooms

    Tear-out through to the trim at the end, including the work behind the walls that nobody sees afterwards.

    • Tubs and showers
    • Tile surrounds and niches
    • Vanities and counters
    • Floors and waterproofing
    • Venting and lighting

  • Kitchens

    Cabinets and counters, with the backsplash tile, flooring and lighting around them handled as part of the same job.

    • Cabinet installation
    • Backsplash tile
    • Counters
    • Flooring
    • Lighting

  • Fixtures and fittings

    Toilets, faucets, shower heads and the smaller fittings, from a straight swap to a full replacement.

    • Toilets and faucets
    • Grab bars and shower heads
    • Curtain rails
    • Blinds and shutters
    • Caulking and insect screens

  • Repairs and small jobs

    The jobs that do not fit a big renovation: a repair, an install, or something that takes an afternoon.

    • Furniture repair and assembly
    • Trim and moldings
    • Door repairs
    • Small electrical
    • General repairs

  • Tile

    Floors, walls and showers, set on a base that has been prepared and levelled first rather than tiled straight over.

    • Floor tile
    • Shower and tub surrounds
    • Backsplashes
    • Tile removal
    • Grout and finishing

  • Flooring

    Plank, tile and the subfloor underneath, repaired and levelled before anything new goes down.

    • Vinyl plank
    • Laminate flooring
    • Tile flooring
    • Subfloor repair
    • Refinishing

  • Drywall and ceilings

    New board, patches and taping, sanded and left ready for paint. Ceilings included, which is where most of the awkward work is.

    • Board and taping
    • Patches and repairs
    • Ceilings
    • Plaster repair
    • Sanding

  • Decks, siding and exterior

    Decks, porches and the framing that carries them, plus the siding, soffit and trim that finish a house on the outside.

    • Decks and pergolas
    • Porches and framing
    • Siding
    • Soffit, fascia and trim
    • Exterior doors
